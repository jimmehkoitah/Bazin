# Taxonomy seed v0

Purpose: the controlled vocabulary the discovery and classification stages start from. Every row is a concept, not a search term; search terms map to concepts. Evidence levels reflect the 2026-09-12 research (H = seen in seller captions or primary pages, M = seen in secondary sources, L = inferred). Native-speaker review is required before this is used for ranking.

## Business-type concepts

| Type | Definition | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| fabric retailer | Sells bazin by the metre, yard or pièce to consumers | Price per metre or yard, "vente en détail", shop address, "promo" |
| fabric wholesaler | Sells in bulk to resellers | "vente en gros", "grossiste", MOQ, "import de Dubai", "usine" |
| brand partner | Getzner-appointed distributor or partner boutique | Getzner stories or press, "Getzner" in trade name with brand acknowledgement |
| tailor / atelier | Makes garments to measure from client fabric or own stock | "sur mesure", fittings, deposits, lead times, atelier address |
| couture designer | Branded collections, own models, often diaspora-facing | Collection names, lookbooks, "disponible sur commande" with price |
| embroiderer | Embroidery service on client garments | "broderie machine", close-ups, per-piece embroidery price |
| ready-to-wear seller | Sells finished garments, often online (Etsy, Afrikrea, Instagram) | Sizes ("du 34 au 50"), shipping, "prêt à porter" |
| marketplace seller | Storefront on Etsy, Afrikrea, Amazon, Jumia | Listing URLs, marketplace ratings |
